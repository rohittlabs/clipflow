HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. That's it.

01

Copy anything

Use Ctrl + C as you normally do. ClipFlow runs quietly in your tray.

→
02

Open ClipFlow

Press Ctrl + Shift + V from anywhere to bring up your clipboard history.

→
03

Pick & paste

Search, select, hit Enter. The item is back on your clipboard, ready to paste.
